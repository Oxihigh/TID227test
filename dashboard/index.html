Office of the Club Growth Director

District 227 Strategic Vision, Mission & Growth Framework

Our Vision

To expand the reach of Toastmasters by building resilient, vibrant clubs and empowering every member to achieve personal and leadership excellence across District 227.

Our Mission

We support club chartering, foster membership retention, promote marketing initiatives, and empower district leaders with actionable performance insights to achieve Distinguished District success.

Strategic Growth Pillars

01

Club Chartering & Extension

Expanding the footprint of District 227 by building strong corporate and community clubs to bring communication and leadership opportunities to new audiences.

02

Membership Building & Retention

Championing Smedley, Talk Up Toastmasters, and Beat the Clock membership campaigns while supporting clubs with membership retention tools.

03

Data-Driven District Leadership

Equipping District 227 Division and Area Directors with live performance analytics to provide targeted support to clubs in need.
